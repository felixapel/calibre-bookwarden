Recall per field

This sample is closed: its results were shown on {{.ClosedAt.UTC.Format "2006-01-02 15:04"}} UTC, and its answers are final. The fixes counted are those the sampled run had when the sample was drawn, under findings version {{.FindingsVersion}} and decision version {{.DecisionVersion}}.

Recall is the share of books you called wrong that Bookwarden has a fix for. If you typed the right value, the fix must propose the same value under the style guide’s equivalences. Skips count nowhere. Each book stands for its stratum’s share of the library, so the figures are reweighted by stratum size. The interval is approximate (effective sample size): a 95 % Clopper-Pearson interval at neff = (Σw)² / Σw² over the wrong books. Unequal weights act like a smaller sample and widen it.
